Battle is turn based. PvP fights are realtime, players have to predict opponents moves.
# Chaos meter
Every battle has a Chaos Meter that is shared between the player and their opponent.  
Once the meter is filled, a random event is triggered.
After event triggered, chaos meter is filling again. Events are stacking - longer battle makes more chaos.
## Chaos fills
- +10 every turn.
- +15 when someone misses.
- +20 when someone crits.
- +25 when a faction ability fails.
- +30 when player or boss drops below 25% HP.
- Bosses can have custom meter triggers.
