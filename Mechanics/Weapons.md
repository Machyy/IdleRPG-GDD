Weapons has their types. Every type has own abilities and passive. Weapons got 2 ablities + basic attack (basic is same for every weapon).

**FOR ALPHA TESTING ONLY 2 TYPES**
# Types
## **Katana**
**Passive:** Crits apply 1 Bleed stack.
### Ablities
#### Quick Draw
- Fast attack.
- Always acts first this turn.
#### Thousand Cuts
- 3 smaller hits. (no crit)
- Extra damage per Bleed stack.
## **Hammer**
**Passive:** Ignore 15% armor.
### Ablities
#### Crushing Blow
- Big damage.
- Applies Shattered (-armor).
#### Earthshaker
- Medium damage.
- Stun chance if target is Shattered
